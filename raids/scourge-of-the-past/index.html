What can I track?

DungeonHub reads publicly accessible Bungie activity reports. Completed activities may include checkpoint completions; a full-start indicator is shown only when explicitly supported by report data. Lowman classifications require a completed raid PGCR with a solo, duo or trio roster and an explicitly confirmed fresh start.

Historical activity can be missing due to API availability, character deletion or privacy settings. We do not fabricate records to fill gaps.
